Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the agent fee?
Our agent fee is 10% of 1 month rent for rentals. For land sales, it's 5% of the selling price. This is standard in Kampala.
Do you accept 1 month or 3 months rent?
Yes, some landlords accept 1 month + deposit. Most prefer 6 months. We will help you negotiate with the landlord.
Are your guest houses available for 1 night?
Yes! We have guest houses from UGX 30,000 per night. Contact us on WhatsApp to check availability.
How do I know the land has a genuine title?
We only list land with verified documents. We advise you to do a search at the Ministry of Lands before payment. We can connect you to a lawyer.
Do you deliver gas?
Yes, we do gas refilling and delivery in Wampewo and Kasangati. Call or WhatsApp us to order.
